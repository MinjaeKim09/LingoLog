Help with LingoLog

Questions about your words, practice, or Daily Stories? Contact Minjae Kim for support.

Email support

Include your app version, iOS version, and what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or private vocabulary.

Restore a subscription

Open More in LingoLog and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple account that originally purchased Daily Stories.

Cancel or manage billing

Use Manage Subscription in LingoLog, or open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting LingoLog does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles payments and refund requests.

Daily story limits

Daily Stories creates one story per subscription per UTC day, shared across languages. If a request is interrupted, retry in the same language to recover that day’s story.

Translation or stories are unavailable

Check your internet connection and your Online features choices in More. For stories, also check your subscription and save at least three words. AI-generated content can contain errors.

Your words and privacy

Your vocabulary, memory cues, and study progress are stored on your device. Export your words from More before changing devices. Reset All Data removes local learning data; it does not cancel purchases or erase previously submitted server data.

To ask about or request deletion of server data, email support. We may need limited purchase information to locate story records, but never your Apple password or payment card number.
