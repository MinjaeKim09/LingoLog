Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

LingoLog is developed by Minjae Kim. This policy explains how the app handles information when you build a vocabulary collection, translate text, practice, or use Daily Stories.

Information on your device

LingoLog stores your display name, vocabulary, meanings, memory cues, language spaces, study progress, saved stories, and preferences locally. You do not create a LingoLog account. Device backups and exported files are controlled by you and your device settings.

Online translation

If you allow online translation, text you type and the selected source and target languages are sent through our Firebase backend to Google Cloud Translation. Translation can happen as you type. Memory cues are not included in translation requests. The backend also retrieves the list of supported languages.

AI stories

If you allow AI story sharing, selected vocabulary, its meanings, and the learning language are sent through Firebase to Google Gemini to generate a story and comprehension questions. Your Apple-signed subscription transaction is sent to our backend to verify access; it is not included in the Gemini prompt.

Our Firestore database stores completed story results, the vocabulary used, the language, dates, and a persistent purchase transaction identifier. These records enforce the daily allowance and let you recover an interrupted request. They are linked to that purchase identifier even though LingoLog does not have user accounts.

Purchases and service security

Apple processes subscriptions. We receive transaction information needed to verify access, such as product identifiers, expiration or revocation information, and transaction identifiers. We do not receive your payment card details.

Firebase App Check and Apple App Attest help verify that requests come from the app. Network services process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and error information. Our translation rate limiter stores a hash derived from the app identifier and client address, request counts, and timestamps to limit abuse.

Providers and purposes

We use Apple for purchases and app attestation, and Google Firebase, Google Cloud Translation, and Google Gemini for hosting, security, translation, and story generation. Processing may occur outside your country. We do not sell your information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

See Firebase privacy information, Cloud Translation data usage, Gemini API terms, and Apple’s privacy policy. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information in vocabulary or prompts.

Retention and your choices

Local data remains until you remove it or reset the app, subject to your device backups. Server story and quota records currently have no automatic deletion schedule. Provider logs and other operational information follow the applicable service retention settings and policies.

You can turn off future translation or AI story sharing in More → Online features. This does not undo requests already processed. Use Reset All Data to clear local learning data, or email us to request access to or deletion of server information. We may need limited purchase details to locate records. Information required for security, legal compliance, or transaction records may need to be retained.

Support and changes

If you contact support, we use your email and the information you provide to respond. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to kim.minjae@nyu.edu. We will update this page when data practices change, and seek new permission when required.
