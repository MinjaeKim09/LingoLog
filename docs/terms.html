Learning and AI features

Translation and AI stories are learning aids. They may contain mistakes, misleading information, or inappropriate output. Check important meanings and facts against reliable sources. Submit only content you are entitled to use, and avoid sensitive personal information.

Daily Stories subscription

Daily Stories is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription. Its localized price and billing period are shown before purchase. It includes one generated story per subscription per UTC day, across learning languages, and access to additional language spaces. An interrupted story request can be retried in the same language to recover the existing result.

Apple charges your account after you confirm a purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings. Deleting the app or resetting its data does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests under its applicable policies.

Availability and local data

Online features require an internet connection and functioning service providers. Your vocabulary and progress are stored locally; use the export feature to keep a separate copy of your words. AI and translation services may be temporarily unavailable.
